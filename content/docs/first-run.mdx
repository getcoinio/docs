## Create your account

The first time you open Coinio it asks for a username and a password of at least 10 characters. This is the only account: Coinio is single-user, and once it exists the setup page is gone. Every later visit shows the login page.

Failed logins are limited to 10 per 15 minutes per IP address. A session lasts 30 days.

## The welcome steps

Right after signing up, three short steps follow. **Skip** in the corner ends them at any point; everything can be changed later in **Settings**.

<Screenshot name="first-run-welcome" alt="The third welcome step: explore with sample data, start empty, or import from Wallos" />

<Steps>
<Step>

**Language.** English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. It starts in your browser's language. This also sets the language of the default notification messages.

</Step>
<Step>

**Currency and time zone.** Your main currency, the one totals are shown in (guessed from your browser's region), and your time zone (taken from your browser). The time zone decides what "today" is, when automatic payments are recorded and when reminders go out.

</Step>
<Step>

**How to start:**

- **Explore with sample data** adds eight example subscriptions priced in your currency. A banner on the dashboard removes them with one click; anything you add yourself stays.
- **Start empty** goes straight to the dashboard.
- **Import from Wallos** opens **Settings › Import**. See [Import and export](/docs/features/import-export).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Add a subscription

Use **Add subscription** in the top bar, or **Quick add** on the dashboard: type a name or a website (`spotify.com`), pick monthly or yearly, done. It starts today; change the rest later. Details in [Subscriptions](/docs/features/subscriptions).

## Before you rely on it

- Set up [backups](/docs/maintenance#back-up) of the data volume.
- If you want reminders, add a channel in [Notifications](/docs/features/notifications): email, Telegram, Discord, ntfy and more.
- For the widget or AI connectors, [expose Coinio over https](/docs/expose).
