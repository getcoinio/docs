Coinio keeps every recurring payment on one page: streaming, cloud storage, the gym, rent, the domain you renew once a year. You see what is due this week, what is left to pay this month and what the whole year costs, converted to your main currency.

It runs as a single container on a machine you control. There is no account on someone else's server, no telemetry and no paid tier.

<Screenshot name="dashboard" alt="The Coinio dashboard with sample data: money left to pay this month, a spending chart and upcoming payments" />

## What it does

- **Subscriptions with real billing cycles.** Weekly, monthly or yearly, every N cycles, paid automatically or by hand. Mark a payment as paid, skip a period or pause a subscription; the history stays.
- **A built-in catalog.** 161 popular services with their websites, so the logo comes by itself. Any other site works too.
- **Calendar and reminders.** Payment days months ahead, and reminders by email, Telegram, Discord, Slack, ntfy, Gotify, Pushover, webhooks or Apprise.
- **Many currencies.** Each subscription keeps its own currency; totals are converted with daily exchange rates.
- **History and reports.** What you paid in the last 12 months and what to expect in the next 12, by category.
- **Import and export.** Bring your data over from Wallos; export subscriptions and payments as CSV or JSON.
- **AI assistants (MCP).** Claude, ChatGPT and other MCP clients can read and update your subscriptions.
- **iPhone widget.** Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets through Scriptable.
- **Your own look.** Themes, accent colour, fonts, dashboard cards, custom CSS and your own HTML cards.

## How it runs

Every install method starts the same image, `ghcr.io/getcoinio/coinio:latest`:

- the web app and API listen on port **8080** inside the container;
- all data lives in **`/data`**: an SQLite database (`coinio.db`) and the downloaded logos;
- one account per install, created the first time you open it.

Back up `/data` and you have backed up everything.
