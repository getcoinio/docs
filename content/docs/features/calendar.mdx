**Calendar** shows one month at a time with each payment on its due day, logos included. Move between months with the arrows; **Today** jumps back.

<Screenshot name="calendar" alt="A month in the calendar with payment logos and amounts on their due days" />

Each payment has a state:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Paid | A payment was recorded, by you or automatically. |
| Skipped | You skipped this period. It counts as nothing. |
| Overdue | A manual payment whose date has passed. |
| Due | The next open payment of a subscription. |
| Upcoming | A future date projected from the billing cycle. |

Click a day to see its payments and mark them paid or skip them from there. The month total at the top is in your main currency; paid amounts use what was really charged when you entered it.

Paused subscriptions are not projected. Future months are calculated from each active subscription's cycle, so a yearly renewal shows up in the right month a year ahead.

## Reminders

The calendar is for looking ahead. To be told when something is due, add a [notification channel](/docs/features/notifications): reminders arrive a few days before each payment, and an alert when a manual payment is late.

The dashboard also has a **This week** strip and an **Upcoming payments** list with filters (automatic, paid by hand, foreign currency, category) and a time range.
