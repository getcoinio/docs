## History

**History** lists every payment record, newest first: paid (by you or automatically), skipped, and undone automatic payments. Filter by date range, subscription and status; the list shows the total paid in your main currency.

From a record you can add the real amount charged or undo it. What undo does:

- **A manual payment** is removed and its due date opens again. If it was the latest one, the subscription's next payment moves back to that date.
- **An automatic payment** stays as *Undone* and does not count in totals. Coinio keeps the record so the automatic job does not create it again.

Each subscription also has its own payment history on its page.

## How totals are calculated

| Figure | What goes in |
| --- | --- |
| Left this month | Overdue manual payments plus everything still due this month. |
| Monthly average | Each active subscription's yearly cost divided by 12. A weekly subscription counts 52 times a year. |
| Yearly total | Each active subscription's cost over a year. |
| Paid this month | Payments recorded for due dates in this month. |

Paused subscriptions and skipped periods count as nothing. Subscriptions whose currency has no exchange rate are left out, and the total says how many ([details](/docs/features/currencies#when-rates-are-old-or-missing)).

## Reports

**Reports** covers 25 months: the past 12, the current one and the next 12.

- **Monthly spending:** past months from what was actually paid, future months projected from active subscriptions, and the current month with both.
- **Paid in the last 12 months** and **expected in the next 12 months**.
- **By category:** the monthly cost of each category.
- **Most expensive:** your five costliest subscriptions per month.
- **Share in foreign currency:** how much of your monthly cost is in currencies other than your main one.

Paid amounts use what your card really charged when you entered it, and the daily rate otherwise.
