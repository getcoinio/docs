Don't want to run a server? Open a free account at [app.getcoinio.me](https://app.getcoinio.me/signup) and start right away. It is the same app you would install yourself, with no ads and no tracking.

## Signing up

Sign up with an email address and a password, or continue with Google or GitHub. With an email address, Coinio sends a confirmation link first; the account opens once you follow it. Your first and last name come from Google or GitHub when you use them, and you can correct them in **Settings › Account**.

Google and GitHub can both be connected to one account, even when they use different email addresses: **Settings › Account › Connected sign-ins**.

## What is the same

Everything in these docs applies: subscriptions, the calendar, reports, appearance, imports from Wallos, the [MCP server](/docs/integrations/mcp) at `https://app.getcoinio.me/mcp` and the [iPhone widget](/docs/integrations/iphone-widget) with `https://app.getcoinio.me` as the server address.

## What is different

- **Reminders by email** go to your confirmed address, at most five a day. Telegram, Discord, Slack, ntfy and the other channels work as usual with your own bots and links.
- **Limits:** 200 subscriptions, 10 notification channels and 10 API keys per account.
- Notification channels and site icons cannot point at private network addresses.

## Your data

- **Download** everything (account, settings, subscriptions and payments) as one JSON file from **Settings › Account**.
- **Delete** your account there too; it is removed with all its data.
- Moving to your own server? [Install Coinio](/docs/installation), then load that file in **Settings › Import**: your subscriptions and payment history come along ([details](/docs/features/import-export#import-from-another-coinio)).

The [privacy policy](https://getcoinio.me/privacy/) and [terms](https://getcoinio.me/terms/) explain what is stored and why. Questions: [support@getcoinio.me](mailto:support@getcoinio.me).
